## Hi, I'm Alexey Babkin

I build [**convert.express**](https://convert.express) — private, pay-as-you-go
Whisper-class audio & video transcription in the browser.

Upload MP3/WAV/M4A/FLAC/MP4/MOV or paste a file, get a transcript in seconds.
10 free minutes daily, €0.02/minute after, €0.50 minimum charge, no subscription
or account required to try. Results could be translated and summarised. Files auto-delete after 24 hours and are never used
for AI training.

### What it runs on

| | |

| Languages | 66 |
